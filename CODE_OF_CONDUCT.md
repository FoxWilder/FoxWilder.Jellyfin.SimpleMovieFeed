# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, constructively and professionally.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Critique ideas and code rather than people.

## Scope

This standard applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions or interactions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Instances of abusive behaviour may be reported privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
